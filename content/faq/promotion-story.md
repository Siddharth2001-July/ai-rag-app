# Siddharth's promotion: Solutions Engineer → Solutions Consultant at Nutrient

In **March 2026**, Siddharth was promoted from Solutions Engineer to **Solutions Consultant** at Nutrient.io. The promotion reflects a 2-year compounding arc, not a single milestone.

## The arc

- **February 2024** — joined Nutrient as a **part-time** Solutions Engineer, while finishing his role at Techbit
- **November 2024** — went full-time as Solutions Engineer
- **March 2026** — promoted to **Solutions Consultant**

## Why the promotion happened

Three things compounded over the SE tenure:

1. **Technical credibility.** A full-stack engineering background (.NET Core, React, Angular, Node.js, Salesforce/Apex) let Siddharth speak the language of client engineers — not just demo a product, but discuss integration architecture and edge cases at the level clients needed.

2. **A self-initiated revenue stream.** Siddharth designed and shipped a **Salesforce Documents Solution** built on Nutrient's existing Web SDK, opening a new client segment for the company. Initiative, execution, and commercial impact in one project.

3. **Client-driven product feedback.** Being in direct contact with prospective clients, he routed their real requirements back to the Product team — moving the company's roadmap toward signal from the market, not just internal assumptions.

## What changes in the Solutions Consultant role

The Consultant role takes a more strategic posture in the sales cycle. Less "answer this specific technical question" and more "guide this client on solution architecture, multi-product workflows, and longer-term platform decisions." Siddharth is still the technical bridge between Sales and the client — but operating earlier and broader in the deal cycle.

## What this confirms about his career bet

The SE → SC progression validates the thesis Siddharth has been working toward: that **engineering depth plus client-facing skill compound** rather than competing. Each makes the other more credible. The promotion is the company's signal that the combination is working — and his next layer is to add **AI Engineering** as a third axis on top, which is what recent hands-on work like this RAG portfolio chat is about.
